LYFQR Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LYFQR (“LYFQR”, “we”, “us” or “our”) accesses, collects, uses, stores and shares information when you use the LYFQR mobile application and related safety services. It applies to Users, Guardians and people who submit or receive a LYFQR emergency request.

Developer/legal entity: LYFQR

Privacy contact: lyfqr.support@gmail.com

Public Privacy Policy: https://www.lyfqr.com/privacy-policy.html

1. Information we handle

The information handled depends on the features you use, the information you enter, and the permissions you grant.

Account and profile information

We handle your LYFQR User UID, QR/account identifier, authentication code, account status and profile details you provide, such as your name, nickname, phone number, email address, blood group, emergency contacts, blood-buddy contacts and social links. Authentication codes and account identifiers are used to verify access and protect your account.

Guardian and safety-contact information

We handle Guardian identifiers and contact details, Guardian-link and handshake information, notification preferences, safety permissions and push-notification tokens. This information is used to maintain the relationships you set up and route relevant safety notifications.

Location, trip and destination information

When you enable location-based features, we may handle precise device location, saved Office or other destination locations, geofence events, trip start/end times, trip status and related location history. Background location may be used while an enabled live-trip or geofence feature is running, including when the App is not visible, if you grant the required Android permission.

SOS, rescuer and incident information

When you use SOS or a rescuer emergency feature, we may handle the alert type, time, incident location, status, the User and Rescuer details supplied for the request, emergency contacts, and related Guardian notifications. If you choose to capture or submit evidence, we may handle incident photographs, audio recordings, location records and associated timestamps.

Documents, photographs and audio

We handle documents and media that you choose to capture or upload, including identity or vehicle-related documents stored in the Vault, incident photographs and audio evidence. Camera or microphone access is used only when you invoke a feature that needs it and grant the relevant permission.

Device, app and technical information

We handle app and device identifiers, Firebase Cloud Messaging tokens, app-integrity signals, permission state and technical or error information needed to authenticate an account, deliver notifications, operate safety services and diagnose failures. The App may store preferences, session information and cached feature data on your device.

2. How we use information

We use information to:

  • create, authenticate and maintain LYFQR accounts;
  • connect a User with the Guardians and safety contacts selected by that User;
  • provide QR-based account access, profile, referral and safety features;
  • monitor a live trip or configured Office/destination geofence and send related alerts;
  • create and deliver SOS, rescuer, Guardian and other requested notifications;
  • store, display and delete documents or incident media at your direction;
  • provide maps, place search and location-related features;
  • protect the service, prevent abuse, troubleshoot errors and comply with legal obligations.

We do not use precise safety location or private Vault documents to serve advertising. We do not sell personal information.

3. Location and permissions

The App requests location, background-location, camera, microphone, notification, phone or other Android permissions only when a feature needs them. You can manage permissions in Android settings. If you deny or later revoke a permission, the related feature may be unavailable or may stop working.

Office geofence monitoring and live location are enabled only through the relevant feature setup and permissions. Location accuracy and availability depend on your device, operating system, GPS, battery, network and third-party services.

4. How information is shared

We share information only as needed to operate the features you use:

  • With your linked Guardians, relevant profile, trip, destination, location and emergency information may be shared so they can receive the safety alerts you enable.
  • With a Rescuer or emergency contact, relevant request details may be shared when an emergency request is initiated or handled.
  • With service providers that process information for LYFQR, including Firebase and Google services used for authentication, database, file storage, Cloud Functions, push messaging, maps and place search.
  • With authorities or other parties when required by law, to protect someone's safety, enforce our Terms or investigate fraud or misuse.

Firebase and Google may process information on infrastructure located in different countries, subject to their service terms and applicable law. We do not make your private profile, location, Vault documents or incident media public.

5. Retention

We keep account and profile information while the account is active and for as long as needed to provide LYFQR, resolve disputes, protect the service and meet legal obligations. Saved trip and location records are retained as needed for the safety features and account history, subject to deletion requests and applicable retention requirements.

You may delete individual Vault documents or other content where the App provides a deletion control. SOS evidence is targeted for cleanup after it is older than 48 hours. The scheduled cleanup runs weekly, so removal may occur several days after the 48-hour threshold and may take approximately up to a week under normal operation. Cleanup timing can vary. Other records may have different retention periods.

6. Account and data deletion

You may request permanent account and associated data deletion through Account Control > Delete My Account Permanently in the App or through our external account-deletion request page: https://www.lyfqr.com/delete-data.html. We will verify that you control the account before processing the request.

After verification, we will delete the LYFQR account and associated personal data from the active systems we control, including Firebase Authentication, Firestore and Firebase Storage. We will also remove or unlink associated Guardian links, trips, notifications and uploaded content where they relate to the deleted account. We will complete verified requests without undue delay.

We may retain a limited amount of information where required by law or reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention or an active dispute. Any retained information will be restricted to that purpose and deleted when the purpose ends. Account deletion is permanent and may stop the QR card, Guardian links and access to safety history or documents. Save any information you need before confirming deletion.

7. Security

We use administrative, technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect information. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep your QR/account credentials private and notify us if you suspect unauthorized access.

8. Your choices and rights

You can manage Android permissions, notification preferences, Guardian notification settings and individual files through the features provided in the App. Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of your information. Contact us using the details below; we may verify your identity before responding.
