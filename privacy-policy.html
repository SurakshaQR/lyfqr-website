Privacy & Security

Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This policy explains how LyfQR may handle information when you use our application and website.

Last updated: October 25, 2026

1. Introduction

LyfQR provides safety, identity, QR-based and related utility features. This Privacy Policy describes the categories of information that may be processed, why it may be used, and the choices available to users.

This policy applies to the LyfQR mobile application and www.lyfqr.com.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features you use and permissions you grant, LyfQR may process:

  • Account information: User identifiers and information needed to authenticate users and provide application access.
  • Location information: Location data used by enabled geofencing, trip-monitoring, emergency or nearby-place features.
  • Emergency information: SOS events, timestamps, status information and information needed to notify designated guardians.
  • Camera and audio information: Images or audio recordings if an enabled emergency evidence feature captures them.
  • Guardian and trip information: Information used to configure trusted contacts and manage safety-related trip events.
  • Diagnostic information: Application errors, geofence events, technical logs and troubleshooting information.
  • Support and subscription information: Information provided when contacting support or using paid features.

The information actually processed depends on your account, application configuration, enabled features and device permissions.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide and maintain application features and account access.
  • Operate SOS and guardian notification functionality.
  • Support enabled location-based safety features and geofencing.
  • Provide QR, identity and nearby-place utilities.
  • Handle emergency evidence when the relevant feature is enabled.
  • Diagnose errors, protect application security and improve reliability.
  • Manage subscriptions, respond to support requests and meet legal obligations.

4. Location and Background Location

Certain safety features may require location permissions, including background location access where necessary for an enabled feature. This may allow the application to detect entry into or exit from a configured office geofence or support trip monitoring.

Nearby-place features may use location to find relevant services. You can manage location permissions in Android settings. Restricting access may limit the functionality of features that depend on location.

Location permissions are requested to support the applicable features, not to provide unrestricted access to location information.

5. Emergency Camera and Audio Features

If an emergency evidence feature is enabled, LyfQR may use camera or microphone access to capture images or audio, subject to device permissions, Android restrictions and the feature's actual operation.

Such evidence may contain sensitive personal information. The application should explain when evidence capture is enabled and how resulting files are stored, shared, retained and deleted.

Evidence storage locations, access permissions, recipients and retention periods must follow the application's actual behavior. Do not assume that all evidence is stored locally or automatically deleted unless that behavior is implemented.

6. Sharing and Service Providers

Information may be shared with designated guardians when needed to deliver a safety feature you configure or activate. LyfQR may also rely on service providers to operate application functionality.

Depending on the services enabled, these providers may include Google Firebase services for authentication, databases, notifications or file storage, and Google Maps or Places services for location-based utilities.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law. The specific data shared with each provider must match the services and integrations actually used by LyfQR.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information should be retained only for as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to applicable legal, security and operational requirements.

Retention periods may differ for account data, trip records, diagnostic logs and emergency evidence. Users should be able to request deletion of eligible personal information through the supported account or support process.

Before this policy is published, LyfQR's actual retention periods, deletion process and any applicable exceptions must be documented accurately.

8. Data Security

LyfQR uses appropriate safeguards intended to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse or disclosure. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

LyfQR's intended audience and treatment of children's information must comply with applicable laws and the application's declared target audience. The application should not knowingly collect children's personal information in a manner prohibited by law.

10. Your Choices and Rights

Subject to applicable law, users may request access to, correction of or deletion of eligible personal information. Users can also manage device permissions through Android settings.

Disabling permissions required by a feature may prevent that feature from working correctly.

11. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when LyfQR's features, data practices or legal obligations change. The current version will be published on the official website.

12. Contact Us

For privacy questions or requests concerning personal information, contact LyfQR:

  • Application: LyfQR
  • Website: www.lyfqr.com
  • Email: lyfqr.support@gmail.com

Please do not send passwords, authentication codes or other sensitive login credentials by email.
