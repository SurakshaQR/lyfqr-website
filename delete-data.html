Account Deletion & Data

This page explains how to request deletion of your LyfQR account information and what happens to your data.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Open the LyfQR app.
  2. Go to the account or profile section.
  3. Select the account deletion option.
  4. Complete the registered email OTP verification.
  5. Follow the instructions shown in the app.

If you cannot access the app, contact LyfQR support using the contact details published on the official website.

What Information Is Cleared?

When an account deletion request is successfully processed, the app is designed to clear applicable personal profile fields, which may include:

  • Name and nickname
  • Registered email address stored in the profile
  • Mobile number
  • Blood group
  • Emergency contact details
  • Blood buddy and social link information
  • Vault-related profile links and applicable document fields

The exact information cleared depends on the deletion process implemented in the app.

Information That May Be Retained

Certain account identifiers, including User UID and Unique Code, may remain stored after the profile information is cleared so the account can continue to be identified by the app.

Other records, such as guardian connections, SOS records, trip information, uploaded files, and technical records, may require separate handling.

Important: Clearing profile fields does not necessarily delete all account-related records. Any retained information should be handled according to the applicable privacy policy and legal requirements.

Need Help?

If you need help with an account deletion request or have questions about your information, please contact LyfQR through the official website.
